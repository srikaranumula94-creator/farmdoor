# 🥬 Farmdoor - Fresh from Farmers, Direct to Your Door

**Farmdoor** is a modern, responsive vegetable e-commerce web platform that connects local farmers directly with consumers—eliminating middlemen markups, delivering sunrise harvests within hours, and ensuring farmers receive fair value for their hard work.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

### 1. 🌾 Brand & Agricultural Theme
- Modern, clean agricultural aesthetic with emerald green, white, and warm earth tones.
- Tagline: *"Fresh from Farmers, Direct to Your Door."*
- Full responsive design optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

### 2. 🔐 Customer & Farmer Authentication
- Sign In and Sign Up for both **Customers** and **Farmer Producers**.
- **1-Click Demo Login Switcher** located directly in the navbar:
  - 🟢 **Customer Demo** (`priya@farmdoor.com`)
  - 🚜 **Farmer Demo** (`ramesh.patil@farmdoor.com`)
  - 👑 **Admin Demo** (`admin@farmdoor.com`)

### 3. 🥦 Vegetable Catalog & Freshness Tracking
- High-resolution fresh produce catalog with realistic Indian market items (*Country Tomatoes, Farm Spinach, Baby Carrots, Ladyfinger, Mountain Potatoes, Nashik Red Onions, English Cucumbers, Weekly Combo Boxes*).
- Harvest timestamp indicators (e.g., *"Harvested today, 5:30 AM"*).
- 100% Organic certified and Direct Farmer Deal tags.
- Detailed vegetable modal with nutritional breakdowns, kitchen storage tips, and farmer origins.

### 4. 🔍 Search & Multi-level Filters
- Real-time instant search by vegetable name, Hindi/local name, or farmer.
- Category pills: *All, Daily Essentials, Leafy Greens, Root & Tubers, Organic Combos, Herbs & Seasonings, Exotic & Gourds*.
- Toggles for *100% Organic Only* and *In-Stock Only*.
- Filter by specific local farm/farmer.
- Sorting options: *Featured / Harvest Fresh, Price (Low to High), Price (High to Low), Rating, and Discount %*.

### 5. 🛒 Smart Cart & Flexible Checkout
- Slide-over Harvest Basket Drawer with free delivery progress meter (Free delivery on orders > ₹249).
- Promo code engine with 1-click copy & apply (`FARMFRESH10`, `FIRSTDOOR`, `FARMERHERO`).
- Multi-step checkout with delivery address and harvest time slot selector:
  - **Morning Sunrise Drop** (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM)
  - **Fresh Evening Drop** (4:00 PM - 7:00 PM)

### 6. 💳 Flexible Payment Options
- **Instant UPI & QR Code** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm simulation).
- **Credit / Debit Cards** (Visa, RuPay, MasterCard).
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** with payment on inspection.
- Confetti celebration upon order confirmation.

### 7. 🚚 Live Order Tracking & History
- Interactive 5-step delivery pipeline:
  1. *Order Placed*
  2. *Harvested at Farm*
  3. *Quality Checked & Eco-Packed*
  4. *Out for Delivery* (with courier partner name, vehicle, phone)
  5. *Delivered*
- Built-in **"Advance Delivery Stage"** prototype simulator button to test real-time tracking progression.
- Customer "My Orders" modal to view active orders, re-order baskets, or cancel placed orders.

### 8. 🚜 Farmer Partner Portal
- Accessible via the **"Farmer Portal"** button.
- Overview of farm earnings, harvest volume (kg), and active crops.
- **Upload New Harvest Form** with 1-click preset vegetables (Green Peas, Beetroot, Ridge Gourd, Mint Leaves) or custom produce details.
- Inventory manager to update prices in real time, toggle in-stock/sold-out status, or delete crops.
- Daily customer demand sheet for morning farm dispatches.

### 9. 🛡️ Admin Operations Hub
- Accessible via the **"Admin Hub"** button.
- Live order dispatch pipeline with 1-click status dropdown updater.
- Global product catalog management (live price edits and deletion).
- Farmer verification management (toggle verified badges).
- Customer directory with spending metrics.
- Daily Mandi wholesale rates and supermarket benchmark editor.

### 10. 💬 WhatsApp Customer Support
- Floating WhatsApp widget with prefilled questions and quick helpline contact (`+91 98765 43210`).

### 11. 📊 Daily Mandi Prices Comparison
- Live ticker and dedicated modal comparing Farmdoor direct prices against supermarket retail prices, showing customer savings of up to 40%.

### 12. ⭐ Customer Reviews & Ratings
- Interactive rating submission form inside the product detail modal.
- Real-time calculation of average product rating and review count.

## 🚀 Running the Project

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start Vite Development Server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```

Open **`http://localhost:5173/`** in your browser to explore Farmdoor.
